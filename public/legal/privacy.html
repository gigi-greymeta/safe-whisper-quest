Privacy Policy

Phantom by GreyMeta · Effective 21 November 2026

Short version. Phantom is an end-to-end encrypted messenger. You do not need an email address, phone number or password to use it. Your messages are encrypted on your device and we cannot read them. The only things our servers hold are your chosen display name, your public key, and encrypted messages that are waiting to be delivered (for at most 7 days). We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data.

1. Who we are

Phantom ("the app", "we", "us") is operated by GreyMeta. You can contact us about privacy at CONTACT_EMAIL. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, and we honour equivalent rights under other privacy laws that apply to you, such as the GDPR.

2. How Phantom works

When you create an identity, the app generates a cryptographic key pair on your device. Your private key never leaves your device. Messages and attachments are encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, with keys agreed using elliptic-curve Diffie–Hellman and a ratchet for forward secrecy) before they are sent. Our relay server only ever handles ciphertext, and it does not have the keys to decrypt it.

3. Data stored on your device

Your identity key pair, display name, contacts, message history, group membership, safety numbers, settings and optional screen-lock data are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. Where you enable a screen lock, this data is kept in an encrypted vault. We have no access to it. The app excludes it from iCloud and Google device backups. Removing the app, using Burn All Data, or using Delete Account erases it.

Vanishing groups (Premium). Messages, photos and videos in a vanishing group are stored on our servers only in encrypted form, under a random group address, for 48 hours from when they are posted, then permanently deleted (our clean-up runs every five minutes, and expired items are never served even before then). The group admin can delete everything sooner by deleting the group, and removing a member deletes the group's earlier messages. Our servers never learn a group's name, who is in it, or who sent what: that information is end-to-end encrypted between members. Group messages are not stored on your device.

5. Data we do not collect

  • No phone number, email address, password or real name is needed.
  • We cannot read your messages, attachments, contact list or group membership.
  • No location, contacts address book, advertising identifier or device identifiers.
  • No payment card or billing details. Apple and Google handle payment.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.

6. Network and infrastructure data

Our relay runs on Supabase, a cloud database and hosting provider. Like any internet service, Supabase receives your device's IP address and basic request information (such as time and request type) when the app connects, and may keep it in short-lived operational logs for security and reliability. We do not use this information to build profiles or to identify you, and we do not link it to your directory entry. Data may be processed outside Australia, depending on where our Supabase project is hosted.

Voice calls. To connect a call through home routers and mobile networks, the app contacts a network-discovery (STUN) or relay (TURN) server run by a service provider, which sees your IP address while the call is set up or relayed but never the call audio, which stays encrypted. When calls are not relayed, the two devices connect directly and each can see the other's IP address. Call setup messages are end-to-end encrypted and travel through our relay like any other message.

7. Device permissions

  • Internet: to send and receive encrypted messages.
  • Camera and photo library (optional): only when you choose to attach a photo to a message, or open the QR scanner to add a contact or check a safety number. The photo is encrypted on your device before it is sent. Scanned QR codes are read on your device; camera images are never stored or sent anywhere. You can refuse this permission and keep using the app.
  • Microphone (optional): only during a voice call you start or answer (Premium), or while you record a voice message (Standard and Premium). Call audio is encrypted between the two devices and is never recorded or stored. Voice messages are end-to-end encrypted like any other message and expire from our relay with them. If you use a voice filter (Premium), your voice is changed on your device before it is recorded or sent, so others only ever receive the filtered voice.
  • Face ID, fingerprint or face unlock (optional): to unscramble a protected message or open your locked notes. The check happens entirely on your device; we never receive biometric data.
  • Location (optional, Premium): read only when you tap "Drop a pin" and choose to share where you are. The pin is end-to-end encrypted to the people in that chat, so we can't see it. The app never tracks your location in the background. "Open in Maps" passes a pin's coordinates to your maps app (Apple Maps or Google Maps) only when you tap it, under that provider's privacy policy.

Notes in the locked notepad are encrypted and stay on your device; they are never sent to our servers. Phantom does not use your contacts.

8. Subscriptions and payments

Phantom has a Free plan and paid Standard and Premium plans, sold in the iOS and Android apps. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never receive your name, payment card or billing address. The app asks the store on your device which plan you have. That status is not sent to our servers or linked to your Phantom identity. The store console shows us aggregate sales information and limited transaction records, such as country, price and date, which we use only for accounting, refunds and legal obligations.

9. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing. We use Supabase only as a service provider to run the relay. Apple and Google process subscription payments, as described above. We may disclose the limited server-side data described above if Australian law requires it. Because messages are end-to-end encrypted, we are not able to provide their content to anyone.

10. Security

We protect data with end-to-end encryption, forward secrecy, message padding, a strict content security policy, encrypted transport (TLS), and short retention periods. No system is perfectly secure. Keep your device and any vault backups you export safe.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Data → Delete Account. See Account Deletion.
  • Delete specific data: see Data Deletion.
  • Access or correction: your data lives on your device and can be viewed or exported from the app. For the server-side data, email us with your key fingerprint.
  • Complaints: contact us first. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or to your local data protection authority.

12. Children

Phantom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
