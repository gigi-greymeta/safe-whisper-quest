Delete your Phantom data

Phantom by GreyMeta · Effective 21 November 2026

You can delete some or all of your data in Phantom (by GreyMeta) without deleting your account. To delete your account and all server data, see Account Deletion.

In the app

To deleteDo thisEffect
A contact and your conversation with themRemove the contact from Contacts or from the chatDeletes the chat, keys and safety number for that contact from your device, and deletes the inbox queue for that contact on our server. Any undelivered encrypted messages in it expire within 7 days.
Messages automaticallySettings → Security → set a disappearing-message timerMessages are deleted from your device when the timer runs out
Everything on this deviceSettings → Data → Burn All Data, or the Reset WizardErases all local data and deletes your inbox queues on our server. Your public directory entry stays until you delete your account.
Everything, instantly, under duressSet up Sentry Mode (Settings → Security), then enter the decoy passcodeSilently erases all local data

Deleted automatically

  • Encrypted messages on the relay: within 7 days of being sent
  • Inbox queues: after 30 days without activity

By email

To delete specific server-side data, such as your directory entry, without using the app, email hello@greymeta.com.au with your key fingerprint or display name. We will act within 30 days and confirm by email.
